# M5.5 cooked environment products and complete standard IBL

Accepted 2026-08-09 on the reference RTX 4090/Core i9-14900K system.

## Changed behavior and architecture

Production startup no longer parses a source HDR image or binds an equirectangular
map. `iridium.environment` schema 1 is a self-contained, source-free cooked product
with a manifest plus AP1 radiance cube, diffuse irradiance cube, GGX-prefiltered
specular cube, and two-channel F0/F90 split-sum BRDF LUT. The manifest freezes the
source texture GUID/primaries/radiance scale, cube face orientation, roughness-to-mip
mapping, convolution implementation, sample sequence, and tool version. Invalid
schema, topology, section, format, payload size, negative/nonfinite FP16 values,
unsupported primaries, or over-range source radiance fail before publication.

The RHI now represents 2D, 2D-array, and cube topology with explicit layer and mip
counts. Render-graph image descriptions and Vulkan allocation/view/upload preserve
those contracts. `RG16_SFloat` was appended as texture-format value 9; explicit
numeric values preserve every serialized M3 BC-format identifier.

`IridiumBakeEnvironment` performs deterministic offline conversion. Radiance and
specular products use the frozen Vulkan +X,-X,+Y,-Y,+Z,-Z convention. Diffuse
irradiance projects every source texel exactly into nine real spherical-harmonic
coefficients using texel-solid-angle basis integrals, then applies the Lambertian
cosine kernel. This removed unacceptable bright-source Monte Carlo variance while
preserving the constant-environment `pi * radiance` oracle. GGX prefiltering and the
F0/F90 BRDF LUT use a deterministic Hammersley base-2 sequence and float32 product
storage with finite FP16 publication.

Deferred and complex-forward consumers share `environment_ibl.glsl`. Diffuse uses
irradiance times `(1-F) * diffuseAlbedo / pi`; specular uses the prefiltered GGX
cube and `F0*A + F90*B`. AO affects indirect light only. The initial product is the
documented single-scatter split-sum model; clearcoat and iridescence reuse it, while
sheen and anisotropy retain explicitly named isotropic GGX approximations. Sky
radiance, irradiance, prefilter, and LUT occupy scene bindings 19, 16, 17, and 18.

Missing products bind semantic neutral resources: black radiance/irradiance/
prefilter cubes and an RG16 `(1,0)` BRDF identity. Cooked replacement uses the M3
runtime publication service. A replacement validates and uploads completely, waits
for all Vulkan frame contexts before updating non-update-after-bind scene sets,
publishes all four descriptors atomically, and retires the previous images through
deferred destruction. Failed preparation or publication leaves the old environment
bound. Capture and profile metadata record the exact environment artifact, asset
and source GUIDs, CookKey, primaries, and radiance scale.

## Bake and convergence evidence

The accepted High product is 512 radiance, 32 irradiance, 256 prefilter, 256 BRDF,
and 1,024 GGX/BRDF samples. It bakes the 8K cobblestone-night source at explicit
scale 0.01 in 91.924 seconds and produces a 21,284,592-byte artifact with SHA-256
`316c6d12f8639940359123aff26d14e8c5be85fe28b03f497cbd1084857a7478`.
Its CookKey is
`b01fbac1ec89e888515d938ba42bf7c0dff3a3fac6b788f197292d2da44f92bc`.

The 256-sample candidate is rejected. Against the accepted 1,024 product its
prefilter normalized RMSE is 5.360% and BRDF normalized RMSE is 0.621%; the BRDF
p99 absolute error is 0.01038. A compact 1,024-versus-4,096 oracle makes radiance
and irradiance byte-exact, measures 5.161% prefilter normalized RMSE with 0.04171
p99 absolute error, and measures 0.171% BRDF normalized RMSE with 0.003174 p99
absolute error. The 4,096 oracle costs 2.63x the compact 1,024 bake. M5.5 accepts
1,024 as the production balance and retains 4,096 as the reference policy; sparse
bright-source prefilter tails remain a deliberate quality-bake investigation, not
a runtime correctness defect.

## Deferred/forward and complex-material evidence

Equivalent standard deferred and alpha-forced-forward fixtures at 3840x2160 are
finite. Scene-linear luma differs by 0.000001188 maximum, 0.00000001448 mean, and
0.0000001121 RMSE. With a 0.01 signal floor, relative p99 is 0.00643% and maximum
is 0.01188%. This is tighter than the M5.4 direct-light gate and shows both paths
consume the same cooked products and equations.

The 4K complex-closure lab, including opaque complex and both transparency buckets,
is finite from 0.0000153 to 0.706543 scene-linear AP1. Its capture SHA-256 is
`c836748e65f8085e58c42f99921431dbd597cf4845f593f870e79aa670f84428`.

## 4K performance and memory

Release measurements use 64 warmups and 64 measured frames. Times are median / p95
in milliseconds.

| Fixture/range | M5.5 time | M5.4 zero-light comparison |
|---|---:|---:|
| standard deferred lighting, direct plus complete IBL | 0.110 / 0.119 | 0.123 / 0.127 |
| forced-forward standard surface | 0.055 / 0.059 | 0.056 / 0.059 |
| complex opaque forward | 0.002 / 0.003 | 0.002 / 0.003 |
| complex background transparency | 0.005 / 0.006 | 0.005 / 0.005 |
| complex foreground transparency | 0.005 / 0.005 | 0.005 / 0.005 |

The full deferred GPU frame is 0.394 ms median / 1.804 ms p95 / 2.993 ms p99.
The forced-forward fixture is 1.756 / 2.166 / 3.092 ms; unrelated UI/output spikes
dominate its total while the lighting ranges remain stable. Complete standard IBL
therefore remains well within the provisional 1.4 ms direct-light-plus-IBL budget.

The resident High product adds 20.297 MiB requested and 20.362 MiB committed over
the M5.4 neutral environment, with 20.297 MiB of startup upload. Environment
creation/upload is 308.1-310.0 ms in Release. An editor hot replacement temporarily
doubles that category to 40.594 MiB requested / 40.722 MiB committed, then returns
to one 20.297 MiB product after deferred retirement. The validation-clean 8,000-
frame replacement published one revision, retired one revision, uploaded 21,282,784
bytes, and dropped zero profile frames. Its Debug publish tick reached 3.594 seconds;
this synchronous editor hitch is accepted for M5.5 correctness and remains a future
asynchronous-publication optimization.

## Verification

- Debug and Release builds complete; both configurations pass 65/65 CTests.
- Product tests cover deterministic serialization, strict sections/layouts,
  corruption, FP16 domain, supported primaries, constant-environment physics,
  repeated-product identity, BRDF finiteness, face centers, and all cube-edge
  orientation pairings.
- Render-graph and Vulkan executor tests cover layered/mipped/cube descriptions and
  views. Shader contract tests cover the shared deferred/forward include and frozen
  M5.5 GLSL/SPIR-V supersession manifest.
- The accepted High product loads in a validation-enabled Vulkan run with six-layer
  cubes and the RG16 LUT. The live 1,024-to-256 product revision also completes with
  Vulkan validation clean after the descriptor lifetime fix.
- Captures and profiles contain the accepted environment GUID, source GUID, CookKey,
  source primaries, scale, and artifact location. Production startup has no source-
  HDR parser fallback.
- Exact machine-readable results and raw-output paths are indexed by
  `docs/performance/data/M5.5-environment-ibl-2026-08-09.json`.

## Remaining risks and deferrals

- The first production product is intentionally single-scatter split-sum IBL.
  Multiscatter energy compensation needs a separately validated model rather than
  an unmeasured shader correction.
- High-dynamic-range GGX prefilter tails remain the reason 4,096 samples are kept as
  a reference bake. Importance sampling/source-mip improvements may reduce offline
  variance in a later cooker revision without changing the runtime product contract.
- Publication currently pays a full frame-context wait and synchronous upload in
  the editor. It is safe and rare, but not a zero-hitch streaming solution.
- M5.8 owns local reflection-probe selection, blending, parallax correction, capture,
  scheduling, and residency. M5.5 supplies the global product/runtime foundation.
- M5.6/M5.7 own shadow visibility; M5.5 does not alter unshadowed direct lighting.

No ADR changed. ADR-0002 already owns scene-linear AP1 and the output boundary;
ADR-0006 already owns shared deferred/forward lighting contracts. M5.6 is next.
