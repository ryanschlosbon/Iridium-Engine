# M7.6 moving occluder, occludee, and disocclusion — 2026-09-11

## Result

The benchmark manifest contract can now move one selected entity by a persistent,
deterministic offset starting at an exact frame. This is fixture behavior, not a
renderer special case. Existing sinusoidal motion remains supported, and benchmark
transforms are now marked dirty only when their evaluated position actually changes.
That lets a step invalidate depth history once and then prove recovery on the next
frame instead of continuously invalidating otherwise unchanged history.

The new frozen `m7-occlusion-motion-manifest.v1.json` contains two four-instance
depth-stack fixtures:

- `m7_occluder_disocclusion_step_v1` moves the nearest instance +6 m on X at
  benchmark-state frame 12, revealing the instances behind it;
- `m7_occludee_reveal_step_v1` moves the farthest initially occluded instance
  -6 m on X at frame 12, making the moving occludee visible beside the stack.

## Correctness evidence

Release synchronization-validation runs at 1280x720 prove the same lifecycle in
both fixtures:

- stable frames before motion consume eligible history and emit exactly 331 of 404
  opaque commands after 73 fused device-owned rejections;
- the motion frame republishes exactly one transform, rejects history as
  `DepthContentChanged`, applies no occlusion rejection, and emits 403 commands for
  the moved-occluder fixture (one independent frustum rejection) or all 404 commands
  for the moved-occludee fixture;
- the immediately following frame consumes the newly published history and emits
  exactly 344 or 345 commands after 59 rejections;
- every eligible frame has exact device/oracle command parity and zero invalid
  results, unsafe mismatches, bin mismatches, or indirect overflow.

Query-only and fused-rejection final-SDR captures are byte-identical both on the
motion frame and the first recovered frame. The moved-occluder pairs have SHA-256
`4b15bcadbc86eeab311128a9fdbd32e7d4018a2f0be5e095fec4f6003ee3cf10`;
the moved-occludee pairs have SHA-256
`9ecc39cc5bdeb091565db44820653d879ab9bc18cfc2e87e5b03483254104fc9`.
The common pre-step capture is distinct, with SHA-256
`5c7cd37994e737d39ac5ff9a47665e5e181f41451fd51f59fc6e942cb3ce40ba`,
which proves that both fixture transitions materially change the image.

## Decision and remaining work

The selected moving-occluder, moving-occludee, and disocclusion correctness gap is
closed for the experimental fused route. This does not admit production rejection.
Small-object and explicit depth-content invalidation are now also complete; see
`docs/performance/M7.6-small-object-depth-content-2026-09-11.md`. Scene-target
resize recovery is complete in
`docs/performance/M7.6-resize-recovery-2026-09-11.md`; duplicate-history removal
is subsequently complete in `docs/performance/M7.6-in-place-view-history-2026-09-11.md`;
the controlled large-occluded-workload performance gate remains.
Production depth-pyramid allocation and command rejection stay off by default.

Debug and Release builds succeed. Both portable suites pass 77/77. No accepted ADR
changed, and M7.6 remains active.
