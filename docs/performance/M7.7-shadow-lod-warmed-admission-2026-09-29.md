# M7.7 conventional shadow-LOD warmed admission

Date: 2026-09-29  
Status: visual/performance gate complete without production admission

## Outcome

Directional cascades, spotlight atlas tiles, and point-cube faces retain their
independent GPU shadow-LOD implementations, but LOD0 remains the production
default. Native-4K visual sweeps show that the current selectors are conservative;
the warmed moving dense-geometry gate does not show enough repeatable benefit to
justify enabling a nonzero global threshold.

The new durable fixture uses eight moving Alfa Romeo instances, one Ultra
directional light, two High spotlights, and two High point lights. Motion refreshes
the conventional shadow consumers after a 120-frame warmup. The fixture measures
600 native-4K frames and uses the existing self-contained cooked LOD artifact.

## Native-4K visual threshold sweep

The static near/mid/far directional, spotlight, and point fixtures were captured at
LOD0 and at 2, 4, and 8 shadow texels. Directional scene-linear output is byte-
identical at every threshold. Spotlight scene-linear output changes at most 72 of
8,294,400 pixels at 8 texels, with maximum AP1 error `3.8146973e-6`. Point output
changes 13 pixels, with maximum AP1 error `9.9182129e-5`; its 2/4/8 captures are
identical to one another. All compared final-SDR captures are byte-identical.

At 8 texels, the static first-refresh command reductions remain small:

| Consumer | LOD0 triangles | 8-texel triangles | Saved | Saved % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Directional | 3,321,626 | 3,291,990 | 29,636 | 0.8922% |
| Spotlight | 905,898 | 892,122 | 13,776 | 1.5207% |
| Point cube | 1,509,830 | 1,486,870 | 22,960 | 1.5207% |

## Moving heterogeneous correctness

The validation-enabled Debug run compares every device command against the
independent CPU oracle across moving refreshes. At 8 texels, every retained frame
reports zero mismatched bins, zero mismatched command regions, and zero overflow.
Per refresh it reduces 90 directional, 80 spotlight, and 140 point-face commands.

The matched frame-300 final-SDR image differs in only 7 of 8,294,400 pixels
(`8.439429e-7`), with maximum code difference 2 and mean luma SSIM
`0.9999999999670409`. The difference is bounded well below a visible admission
concern for this workload.

## Warmed Release timing

Two 120-warmup/600-measured native-4K pairs ran in LOD0/LOD8 then LOD8/LOD0 order.

| Metric | LOD0 A | LOD8 A | LOD8 B | LOD0 B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Wall average (ms) | 9.891658 | 9.404321 | 8.686311 | 8.638779 |
| CPU frame median (ms) | 9.6568 | 8.9400 | 8.5200 | 8.4500 |
| GPU frame median (ms) | 6.482432 | 5.702656 | 5.500416 | 5.509120 |
| Combined shadow GPU median (ms) | 0.961056 | 0.949744 | 0.945152 | 0.952160 |
| Combined shadow CPU record median (ms) | 0.3412 | 0.3544 | 0.3377 | 0.3129 |

The candidate removes 245,136 of 19,325,824 combined shadow triangles per refresh
(`1.26844%`). Combined shadow GPU time improves by only `0.007008-0.011312 ms`.
CPU shadow recording regresses by `0.0132-0.0248 ms`, and whole-frame wall order
reverses between the two pairs. Persistent and transient memory are unchanged.

## Decision

Do not promote an 8-texel or lower global threshold. The visual contract is strong,
but the current cooked chains expose too little removable shadow work, and selector
overhead offsets much of the raster saving. Keep the controls available for explicit
experiments and retain LOD0 by default. Future reconsideration requires materially
broader LOD coverage or a shadow workload with a repeatable complete-path win.

This does not weaken alpha-clip or two-sided caster semantics. The warmed fixture
does not claim transparent-shadow support, and virtual shadow maps remain M7.8.
Position/opacity-only streams and static membership caching remain independent M7.7
optimization candidates.
