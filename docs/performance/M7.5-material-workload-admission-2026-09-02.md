# M7.5 Material-Workload LOD Admission — 2026-09-02

## Decision

Accept generated main-view LOD as a **workload-selectable** M7.5 route and mark the
M7.5 slice complete. The frozen 256-instance stress fixture produces enough opaque
raster work for bounded 2-pixel LOD selection to pay for itself: it removes
12,650,344 main-view triangles (24.5102%), reduces the median complete-frame wall
average by 0.310995 ms (1.4487%), and improves complete GPU median and tails.

Do not change the global default. The smaller frozen three-asset near/mid/far fixture
remains an authoritative negative control: its 2.8282% triangle reduction did not
produce a complete-path win. Projects and quality policy may select the generated-LOD
route for geometry-dense workloads; LOD0 remains the high-fidelity and small-workload
fallback. The existing per-content maximum LOD keeps hero assets pinned when needed.

This is an admission of the parent-contained runtime/cooking route, not the M7.9
streaming architecture. Independent child CookKeys, partial vertex/index residency,
and asynchronous arrival/eviction remain M7.9 work. Independent shadow/probe LOD
remains M7.7 work. No ADR changes are required; ADR-0014's generation-safe identity,
publication, and fail-visible residency contracts remain authoritative.

## Compared paths and frozen workload

The matched routes use the existing frozen `m7_many_instance_stress_v1` fixture and
`m7_many_instance_stress_camera_v1`: a 16 x 1 x 16 grid of 256 dense Alfa instances.
Both paths use the same topology-transactional cooked artifact, Release, native
3840x2160 rendering, no reconstruction, floor-zero residency, no Vulkan validation,
CPU/GPU profiling, 500 warmup frames, 10,000 measured frames, and the same
experimental LOD-history allocation. The only timing variable is maximum LOD zero
versus the 2-pixel, maximum-LOD-15 device selector. Qualification-oracle work is off
for timing.

Five fresh processes per route run in alternating order:

1. LOD0, then 2 px;
2. 2 px, then LOD0;
3. LOD0, then 2 px;
4. 2 px, then LOD0;
5. LOD0, then 2 px.

Four matched pairs favor the candidate by 0.466049, 0.488946, 0.711905, and
0.470360 ms. The third pair reverses by 0.975848 ms. The retained reversal prevents
claiming universal dominance; route medians, complete GPU stages, and improved tails
still establish a material stress-workload win.

## Disposition and remaining work

- M7.5 is accepted as workload-selectable. LOD0 remains the global and hero-quality
  fallback; generated LOD remains explicit policy rather than an unconditional route.
- M7.6 becomes active for conservative Hi-Z occlusion and the shared depth-pyramid
  contract.
- M7.7 still owns independent main/shadow/probe visible sets and shadow-specific LOD.
- M7.9 still owns child-product externalization, progressive geometry residency, and
  the measured parent-key texture-recook defect.
- Twelve eligible opaque primitives containing 6,838 triangles still lack an
  accepted generated level. That content limitation is explicit and fail-visible.
