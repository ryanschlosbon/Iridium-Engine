# M5.11 Production Qualification — 2026-08-13

Status: accepted with one measured allocation-efficiency carry-forward.

## Final production cutover

- Authored Light v2 records and the shared clustered assignment are the only
  production direct-light source for deferred and complex-forward shading.
- Runtime environment lighting is source-free and accepts cooked
  `iridium.environment` products; source HDR decoding remains importer/cooker work.
- The stale `MATERIAL_COMPLEX_FIXED_LIGHT_M5` compiler diagnostic was replaced by
  the truthful shared-clustered-lighting diagnostic. Diffuse-transmission now names
  M6 refraction/volume transport rather than claiming general lighting is pending.
- `m5_11Acceptance` in `assets/benchmarks/m5/run-manifest.v1.json` freezes the Light,
  Sky, reflection-probe, shadow-policy, baked-lighting, and final fixture hashes.
- The local sample car now has a deterministic, source-root-safe acceptance
  manifest and three-light rig. It loads a self-contained cooked 118-primitive,
  87-material, 76-texture-view model and the cooked Belfast 4K HDRI.
- The profiler's bounded counter capacity increased from 128 to 192 after final
  audit proved M5 emitted 144 counters and silently dropped the last 12. The final
  run retains allocation counters and has zero counter overflow.

warm-up frames, 10,000 measured frames, Ultra PCSS, the cooked car, cooked 4K HDRI,
and the deterministic three-owner rig. There were zero frame and counter drops.

The M4 car was an intentionally undressed preservation fixture: 1.5662 ms GPU,
2.8883 ms CPU, 897.558/972.257 MiB requested live/peak, and
967.567/1,042.265 MiB committed live/peak. The M5 result is not a same-content
regression comparison: it adds a cooked 4K environment, three authored shadow
owners, PCSS, clustered assignment, complex-forward lighting, and 528 MiB of
explicit shadow-map capacity. Even so, the dressed GPU p99 is 4.521 ms, leaving
5.479 ms inside the 10 ms base-render target on the reference system.

The 39-call/5,288-byte steady allocation result is constant across every retained
frame in all five processes, with stable live/peak Vulkan totals, so it is bounded
scheduling/diagnostic churn rather than growth or a leak. It does not block M5's
fidelity/performance target, but later renderer work should move the remaining
per-frame vectors to persistent frame-context scratch and restore the M4 zero-call
standard. Final profiles are under
`out/acceptance/m5/final-car-profiler-fixed/`.

## Deliberate deferrals

- Bloom is not implemented; `bloom-hook` remains explicitly skipped and owns no
  image, dispatch, setting, or frame cost. A future implementation must use a
  backend-neutral post-process policy/volume before the final output transform.
- M6 owns general transparency/refraction and colored translucent shadow
  transmission. M10 owns GTAO/CACAO, specular occlusion, physical atmosphere/clouds,
  GI solvers, and baked-product consumption. M11 owns hybrid RT visibility.
- Sparse virtual shadow pages, optional screen-space contact detail, and temporal
  reconstruction remain measured successors, not hidden M5 claims. Conventional
  maps and fixed PCF remain deterministic fallbacks.
