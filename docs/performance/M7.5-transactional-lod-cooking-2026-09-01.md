# M7.5 Transactional LOD Cooking — 2026-09-01

## Outcome

M7.5 now has two additional, explicitly opt-in deterministic cooking policies for
authored meshes that defeated the conservative generated-LOD path:

- boundary-fan splitting repairs vertices that are shared by otherwise disconnected
  open triangle fans in the generated-child working copy; and
- transactional orientation/correspondence validation rejects only an unsafe merge
  candidate instead of discarding an otherwise useful completed LOD level.

Combined with the earlier bounded boundary-edge policy, the representative Alfa
Romeo asset grows from 10 to 57 generated chains and from 18,491 to 180,628 chained
base triangles. In the native-4K three-car lit fixture, main-view triangle reduction
improves from 0.4560/1.3504/2.4549% to 6.9988/13.4023/20.1350% at the unchanged
2/4/8-pixel error ceilings. GPU selection remains exactly equal to the CPU oracle,
and LOD0 remains byte-identical to the locked baseline.

This is a successful experimental coverage result, not production admission. The
new policies and runtime selector remain disabled by default. The short image runs
are correctness evidence rather than timing evidence, 25 sufficiently large opaque
primitives still produce no accepted level, and broader multi-asset motion and
performance qualification remains open. M7.5 therefore stays active.

## Contract and implementation

Importer version 8 accepts the following optional settings when
`generate_lods=true`:

```json
{
  "generate_lods": true,
  "lod_allow_boundary_collapse": true,
  "lod_split_boundary_fans": true,
  "lod_transactional_orientation": true
}
```

Each policy is independently opt-in and omitted from default normalized settings.
Omitting the keys preserves historical settings bytes, CookKeys, and generated
products. Importer version 7 rejects the new keys, and importer 8 rejects them when
LOD generation is disabled or their value is not Boolean. The representative
combined settings therefore receive a distinct settings hash and CookKey.

`ModelLodGeneratorAbiVersion` advances to 4. Boundary-fan repair finds disconnected
incident triangle fans deterministically and duplicates the exact source vertex for
each additional fan only in the generated-child input. It does not rewrite the
canonical primitive, LOD0 vertex/index stream, material identity, or RT geometry.

Transactional merge validation builds a tentative local replacement for each edge
or compatible merge group. It requires compatible representative attributes,
preserved local triangle orientation with a cosine floor of 0.5, and retained local
source-to-reduced vertex/edge correspondence. An unsafe candidate is skipped while
other candidates continue. The completed level must still pass the existing global
orientation, correspondence, displacement-budget, disconnected-island, and equal
component/topology-signature checks. No quality guard was relaxed.

The generator now optionally reports deterministic primitive- and triangle-weighted
outcomes, source-topology failure reasons, edge-gate counts, and whole-level rejection
counts. `IridiumInspectCookedModel` always reports LOD coverage and can reconstruct a
canonical product for analysis without changing the artifact:

```text
--lod-generation-analysis locked|boundary|boundary-fans|transactional
```

The importer also publishes summary information diagnostics and telemetry for cook
callers that retain informational diagnostics.

## Root-cause analysis

The permanent analysis path replaced speculation about primitive fragmentation with
measured rejection reasons. The representative artifact has 174 canonical
primitives and 304,540 triangles. Of these, 113 primitives and 301,966 triangles are
opaque triangle candidates; 29 are below the 64-triangle generation minimum and
account for only 597 triangles.

Under the locked policy, 56 candidates containing 246,894 triangles fail source
topology validation:

| Source topology failure | Primitives | Triangles |
|---|---:|---:|
| Boundary degree | 54 | 241,982 |
| Nonmanifold edge | 1 | 3,485 |
| Inconsistent edge orientation | 1 | 1,427 |

The boundary-degree failures are predominantly otherwise-manifold open fans that
share authored boundary vertices. Boundary-fan splitting repairs all 54 of those
source failures. The two genuinely unsafe primitives remain rejected. With all
three opt-ins enabled, the generator reports:

- 57 generated chains covering 180,628 base triangles and 95 child levels;
- 877 split boundary-fan vertices and 939 exact working-copy duplicates;
- two invalid-source-topology primitives containing 4,912 triangles;
- 25 no-accepted-level primitives containing 115,829 triangles;
- 21,503 locally orientation-rejected and 48,218 locally
  correspondence-rejected merge candidates;
- zero final orientation or correspondence level rejections; and
- 35 final topology-signature level rejections.

The chained bases are 50.44% of opaque primitive candidates and 59.82% of their
triangles, compared with 9.17% triangle coverage in the prior boundary-only cook.
The deepest available children contain 116,589 triangles, a 35.45% reduction within
participating chains and 21.03% relative to all canonical triangles. Those deepest
figures are capacity diagnostics; the camera-dependent selector is measured below.

Every experimental row records 303 device commands, 303 oracle commands, zero

p99 per-pixel RGB code deltas remain zero. The LOD0 capture SHA-256 is
`1ef18ecfaf1afdf52b61c4cc1bfde90325c2d3cecd4c4e038fe1fa53e976ccff`,
byte-identical to the prior locked and boundary-only baselines. Visual inspection of
the full frames, native-pixel near-car crops, and 8-pixel heatmap found differences
localized to fine silhouette and specular samples, with no obvious hole, broken
silhouette, or material discontinuity.

The maximum code delta rises from 91 in the boundary-only result to 207. It affects a
sparse subset of the frame—4,227 pixels, or 0.05096%, at the deliberately permissive
8-pixel diagnostic setting—and is recorded rather than converted into a looser
production threshold. The 2-pixel setting supplies a materially stronger 7.00%
reduction with 0.01077% changed pixels, but it also remains experimental pending
temporal and broader-content qualification.

The six-frame capture runs do not support a frame-time claim. Their purpose is exact
selection/counter validation and deterministic image comparison. A production
performance decision still requires the milestone's fresh-process representative
interval and p95/p99 protocol.

No ADR is superseded. This is an additive asset-cooking policy within the accepted
M7 parent-contained LOD architecture. Default cooking and runtime rendering remain
LOD0. Physical missing-fine residency and fine-grained child CookKeys remain M7.9;
independent shadow/probe LOD and the recorded directional-shadow quality work remain
M7.7. The next M7.5 work should target the remaining no-accepted-level/topology
rejections and then run multi-asset temporal and fresh-process performance admission,
without relaxing the screen-space error ceiling.
