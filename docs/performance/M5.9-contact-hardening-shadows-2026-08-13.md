# M5.9 Contact-Hardening Shadows — 2026-08-13

Status: accepted M5.9 bounded spatial slice. M9 still owns temporal reconstruction
and denoising for stochastic visibility.

## Behavior and architecture

- Directional, spot, and point shadows perform raw-depth blocker search followed by
  source-size-driven PCSS filtering. Directional emitters use angular diameter;
  local emitters use the persisted meter radius already present in the shared
  64-byte light record.
- Zero source extent converges to a hard comparison. Low quality and explicit fixed
  mode use the accepted deterministic 5x5 tent PCF fallback.
- Medium/High/Ultra/Cinematic profiles bound blocker samples at 8/12/16/24, filter
  samples at 16/24/32/48, and penumbra radii at 12/24/48/64 texels. Per-light
  quality clamps the project ceiling.
- Project Settings exposes filter mode, quality ceiling, directional source
  diameter, maximum penumbra, map/pool capacity, owner/update, cache, and texel
  budgets. Command-line bake-off controls are `--shadow-filter` and
  `--shadow-directional-source-diameter`.
- The shared deferred, complex-forward, and reflection-probe capture consumers use
  the same GLSL visibility functions. Every shadow remains owned and applied per
  light, so independent overlapping lights continue to compose correctly.
- Backend-neutral contracts freeze conventional/virtual/ray-traced representation,
  scalar versus RGB transmittance, virtual page policy, temporal inputs, GTAO/CACAO,
  bent normals, specular occlusion, and deterministic raster fallback handoffs.

Each process used 100 warmup and 300 measured frames on the RTX 4090 reference

and maximum process median.

Ultra PCSS adds 0.208896 ms to deferred lighting and 0.206976 ms to the full GPU

than unconditional work. Profiles are under `out/benchmarks/m5.9/`.

## Memory and deferrals

Contact hardening reuses existing D32 maps and therefore adds no shadow-image or
transient-image allocation. Expanded per-frame filter metadata adds 20,160 bytes of
persistent uniform storage across the two frame contexts: 192 directional bytes,
16,384 spot bytes, and 3,584 point bytes.

Sparse virtual pages remain M7, RGB translucent transmission and production AO
remain M10, temporal stochastic reconstruction remains M9, and ray-traced area
shadows/RTAO remain M11. Fixed PCF and conventional maps remain the deterministic
fallback throughout those transitions.
