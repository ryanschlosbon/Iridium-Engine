# M7.5 Transactional-Topology LOD Cooking Checkpoint — 2026-09-01

## Decision

Keep the existing boundary-fan repair and transactional orientation/correspondence
checks, and add a separately opt-in topology-preserving edge-contraction gate. The
new policy is selected with `lod_transactional_topology=true`; default cooking still
does not generate LODs and none of the experimental generation options are silently
enabled.

For every candidate contraction, the generator evaluates the current quotient-mesh
vertex links before committing the merge. The common link of the two endpoint groups
must equal the edge link, with one opposite vertex for a boundary edge or two for an
interior edge. This prevents handle/component/boundary-changing contractions early,
while the existing post-level manifold, connected-component, Euler-characteristic,
boundary-loop, orientation, correspondence, and geometric-error proofs remain the
final authority. `ModelLodGeneratorAbiVersion` is now 5.

This is still experimental M7.5 asset policy. It does not change runtime LOD
selection, default LOD0 rendering, RT geometry, or the accepted parent-contained LOD
architecture. No ADR is superseded.

## Automated contract

`ModelProductTests` now combines a reducible planar grid with a valid seven-vertex,
fourteen-face closed torus. The input is manifold and consistently oriented, the
planar component still produces useful deterministic LOD levels, and the torus
forces at least one topology-changing contraction to be rejected before commit.
Repeated generation must produce byte-equal levels and equal weighted statistics,
with nonzero `topologyRejectedEdgeCount` and zero final topology-rejected levels.

The parent-product test also freezes the canonical contract explicitly: generated
children must preserve the exact canonical vertex and index prefixes, materials, RT
position/index streams, and every base-primitive field except the intentional link
to the LOD section.

`IridiumInspectCookedModel --lod-generation-analysis topology-transactional` exposes
the permanent per-budget outcomes and merge-gate counters. Invalid mode diagnostics
list this mode rather than omitting it.

## Coverage and rejection analysis

The schema-7 artifact has 174 canonical primitives and 304,540 canonical triangles.
Of these, 113 opaque triangle primitives contain 301,966 triangles. The new cook
generates 70 chains and 118 child levels covering 289,619 base triangles, up from 57
chains and 180,628 covered triangles in the preceding transactional checkpoint.

Only 12 otherwise eligible primitives / 6,838 triangles now fail to produce a level:
five / 445 triangles terminate with no reduction and seven / 6,393 triangles remain
below the 10% minimum reduction. Two source primitives / 4,912 triangles remain
invalid before simplification: one non-manifold primitive / 3,485 triangles and one
inconsistently oriented primitive / 1,427 triangles.

Across all budgets the generator records 1,656,732 edge considerations, 203,246
accepted merges, 1,716 topology-rejected merges, 20,242 orientation-rejected merges,
and 48,216 correspondence-rejected merges. The final guards reject zero levels for
orientation, correspondence, invalid reduced topology, or topology-signature change.
Accepted levels are 46 at 0.5% relative error, 50 at 2%, and 22 at 5%.
