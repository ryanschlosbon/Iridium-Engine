# M7.5 Physical Coarser-Resident Fallback — 2026-09-02

## Decision

Accept the parent-contained physical fallback qualification seam for M7.5. A new
qualification-only residency floor can omit fine LOD index ranges before Vulkan
allocation and rebind the canonical primitive to the first retained coarser range.
The primitive, source, material, pipeline, and asset identities remain canonical;
missing fine data therefore remains drawable without a direct-render fallback or a
publication identity break.

The default floor is zero, so production behavior and memory are unchanged. This is
not the M7.9 streaming implementation: the parent vertex stream remains resident and
the floor is fixed at model load. M7.9 still owns child CookKeys, independently
resident vertex/index products, asynchronous arrival/eviction, budgets, and fenced
publication transitions.

No ADR changes. The seam implements ADR-0014's missing-residency fail-visible rule
without changing the accepted GPU-scene ABI.

## Architecture and controls

- `applyRuntimeModelLodResidencyFloor` validates the runtime chain-to-arena mapping,
  chooses `min(requested floor, last cooked level)` per chain, compacts the retained
  UInt16/UInt32 index streams, and omits all finer ranges.
- The stable level-zero primitive identity is retained, but its range refers to the
  first resident coarse payload. Later coarser children remain linked and selectable.
  Bounds and material semantics remain those of the canonical primitive.
- `ModelLodChain::residentBaseLevel` and `ModelAsset` residency statistics preserve
  provenance. Startup emits `IRIDIUM_LOD_PHYSICAL_FALLBACK`; frame profiles expose
  requested maximum resident base, fallback chains, withheld ranges, and withheld
  bytes.
- `--gpu-lod-minimum-resident-level N` requests the qualification floor (`0..15`).
  Capture/profile quality provenance records `_resident_floor_N`.
- The same compacted arena feeds GPU-scene indexed rendering, classic/direct draws,
  forward consumers, transparency, and selection. No consumer can accidentally bind
  the absent fine range.

## Matched native-4K evidence

Evidence is under
`out/benchmarks/m7.5/physical-residency-2026-09-02`. Both runs use the frozen
topology-transactional Alfa artifact, `m7_three_dense_near_mid_far_v1`, Release,
native 3840x2160, Vulkan validation, 12 warmup plus six measured frames, capture frame
four, 2-pixel experimental LOD allocation, and maximum device LOD zero. Pinning the
device selector isolates physical floor zero versus physical floor one.

Floor one applies to 70 chains, omits 70 fine ranges, and withholds exactly 1,737,714
index bytes. The requested and committed process-memory reductions are respectively
1,737,714 and 1,737,712 bytes (the two-byte difference is allocator alignment).
Vertex residency is intentionally unchanged at 46,175,760 bytes.

The floor-one capture is complete and non-constant. Against floor zero, 11,932 of
8,294,400 pixels change (0.143856%), maximum code delta is 34, and mean luma SSIM is
0.9999913623. This is expected bounded geometry loss from deliberately withdrawing
the finest level, not missing geometry. The comparison report SHA-256 is
`5147a0012a9bad500faf7eb65f86b37be9d07808c6e162c475bab03747c16b22`.
The floor-zero and floor-one image SHA-256 values are
`3b14f0b93d0b2714ed045da0974ac0b0e44775ae807629dd750a608e32a26a92`
and `b119e36d7cdba5a40c12563a711ffebbb85e5cfee0b25c7320fc96f98dbd933e`.

The two six-frame averages (5.208583 ms and 5.226900 ms) are functional evidence,
not a performance admission sample. The preceding five-process LOD admission result
remains authoritative for production disposition.

## Remaining work

- Keep generated LOD disabled by default until a materially beneficial workload wins
  the frozen production gate.
- M7.9 must replace this fixed parent-contained floor with independently cooked and
  budgeted child products, partial vertex residency, asynchronous upload/eviction,
  and atomic revision transitions.
- M7.7 still owns independent shadow/probe LOD policy; M7.6 owns Hi-Z.
