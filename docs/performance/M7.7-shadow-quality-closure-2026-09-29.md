# M7.7 conventional-shadow quality closure

Date: 2026-09-29  
Status: accepted; M7.7 complete

## Outcome

The final conventional-shadow quality matrix is frozen by
`assets/m7-shadow-quality-closure-manifest.v1.json`. It reuses the authored M5
contact scene rather than introducing favorable replacement geometry: a thin warm
blocker, a fully clipped alpha-mask blocker, and a double-sided neutral receiver.
Three spaced copies produce nine GPU-scene primitives, exceeding the tiny-workload
threshold, while a 120-frame camera oscillation sweeps from near to far cascade
density regions under the Ultra directional-shadow preset.

This fixture closes the remaining M7.7 thin/alpha/two-sided and camera-motion
evidence gap. It does not change shadow bias, filtering, cascade distribution, or
the production LOD0 policy.

## Correctness evidence

- Validation-enabled Debug completed 120 measured moving-camera frames with the
  independent shadow qualification oracle.
- Directional output varied from 18 to 36 device commands as conservative cascade
  visibility changed. The CPU oracle matched every command; mismatched bins,
  overflow commands, direct fallback, and fallback reason remained zero.
- The stable shadow membership cache hit on every measured frame.
- Alpha-mask shadow draws varied from 6 to 12 with cascade visibility, proving the
  opacity path remained exercised rather than optimized out.
- Native-4K automatic and conventional-direct captures are byte-identical at both
  sweep extremes:
  - far phase: `f206a2875f0c1a08d64fe3ff7faa351abc570569d49c9605e9e804ca324458de`
  - near phase: `1329f1cdf1b43ab95251c1d416fde8c303df66ec75993a64fb81c80b1401e2db`
- Visual inspection at both phases shows one finite attached shadow per visible
  warm blocker and no residual caster or shadow from the fully clipped alpha-mask
  primitive. The near capture retains clean contact and silhouette behavior without
  the old nearest-depth penumbra staircase, objectionable acne, or detached
  peter-panning.

Evidence is retained under
`out/benchmarks/m7.7/shadow-quality-closure/`. The Debug oracle profile is
`debug-oracle-v2.jsonl`; the four native-4K comparison directories are
`automatic-far-4k`, `direct-far-4k`, `automatic-near-4k`, and `direct-near-4k`.

## M7.7 completion mapping

- Camera-invisible shadow/probe membership and independent consumers were accepted
  in the caster-visibility and probe-submission checkpoints.
- Relevant-caster scaling, bounded device commands, stable membership caching, and
  the opaque position-only fetch were accepted by their measured gates.
- Practical/log cascade splits, nearer world-units-per-texel density, bilinearly
  reconstructed PCSS comparisons, and active filter/bias telemetry were accepted
  by the density/filter and receiver-bias checkpoints.
- The new moving fixture closes thin, fully clipped alpha-mask, double-sided,
  close-contact, grazing, peter-panning, and cascade-motion coverage while matching
  the conventional direct reference exactly.
- Shadow and probe LOD experiments remain available but default to LOD0 because
  their warmed gates did not demonstrate a repeatable complete-path benefit.

M7.7 is accepted without superseding an ADR. M7.8 becomes active for the sparse
Virtual Shadow Map production candidate; conventional maps remain the required
selectable reference and fallback.
