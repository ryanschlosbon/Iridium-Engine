# M7.5 Lit LOD Admission Fixture — 2026-08-31

## Outcome

M7.5 now has an additive, explicitly lit native-4K LOD qualification fixture.
Benchmark manifests may declare typed directional, point, and spot lights with
complete transform, photometric, shadow, cone, radius, quality, and priority data.
New evidence therefore does not depend on editor defaults or hard-coded fixture IDs.
Legacy manifests and their frozen hashes were not changed.

The initial 2/4/8-pixel sweep proves very small image error and exact device/oracle
parity, but it also shows that the current conservative generated chain removes too
little work from this asset for production admission. Experimental device LOD remains
disabled by default. The next M7.5 engineering target is stronger deterministic,
bounded-error cooking and coverage diagnostics, not a looser default threshold.

## Fixture and architecture

`assets/m7-lod-lit-admission-manifest.v1.json` defines three separated cars under a
warm shadowed spot key, cool point fill, shadowed directional rim, and a low constant
environment. Its SHA-256 is
`9f1a4441c5afd3fb43f8b25c13486ef55b1383f02b5d6a257bff41b1d550d648`.

`BenchmarkManifest` owns the backend-neutral fixture contract. `Application` maps
the parsed values into normal runtime `TransformComponent` and `LightComponent`
instances. Command-line cluster/light-table stress still overrides fixture lights.
Capture and profile quality settings record the explicit fixture-light count; normal
lighting, clustered assignment, directional/spot shadow ownership, and GPU upload
paths remain unchanged.

The real validation capture reports three active lights, one active 4096x4096
four-cascade Ultra directional shadow, one allocated Ultra spot shadow, a populated
procedural environment, and nonconstant finite signal. Visual inspection confirms
three distinct lit cars with material highlights and self-shadowing.

All four Release runs use the same schema-7 cooked artifact, 3840x2160 native
rendering, 12 warmup frames, six measured frames, capture frame four, ACES 2 SDR at
+2 EV, validation, GPU profiling, and the same fixture. These short capture runs are
correctness diagnostics, not timing evidence.

Every experimental row has zero device/oracle command mismatch and zero visibility
overflow. The comparison uses the engine's fixed-window, sRGB-decoded linear Rec.709
luma SSIM. Local maximum deltas occur on a tiny number of silhouette/highlight pixels;
p95 and p99 per-pixel deltas remain zero. These results demonstrate fidelity headroom,
not a performance win: even 8 px eliminates only 12,320 main-view triangles, and the
complete frame still renders independent LOD0 shadow/forward consumers.
